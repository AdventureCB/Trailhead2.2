TRAILHUB

Delete your account

Trailhub by Lone Peak Overland · Android package com.lonepeakoverland.trailhub · iOS bundle com.lonepeakoverland.trailhub

IN THE APP (IMMEDIATE)

  1. Open Trailhub and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile (bottom right) → the Settings gear.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

BY REQUEST

If you can't sign in, email team@lonepeakoverland.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Trailhub account”. We'll verify it's you and delete the account within 7 days, and confirm by email.

WHAT IS DELETED

Your login, profile (name, handle, photo, bio, phone), posts, comments, likes, follows, trip reports and plans, builds, forum threads and replies, camping spots you added, direct messages you sent, notifications, push-notification registrations, saved items, gear-drop runs, bounty submissions, and any drawing or interest-list signups.

WHAT IS KEPT

Records we're required or allowed to keep for a limited time: ambassador commission and payout records and bounty payout records (tax and accounting, up to 7 years), content-moderation logs, and anonymized analytics that no longer identify you. Public content you posted may have been featured in Lone Peak Overland marketing under the Terms of Service before deletion; we stop using it going forward. See the Privacy Policy for details.
